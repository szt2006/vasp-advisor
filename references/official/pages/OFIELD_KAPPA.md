Description: The tag {{TAG|OFIELD_KAPPA}} sets the strength of the bias potential in the Interface pinning method. 
----
The bias potential in the Interface pinning method is written as
::U_\text{bias}(\mathbf{R}) = \frac\kappa2 \left(Q_6(\mathbf{R}) - A\right)^2 .
The tag {{TAG|OFIELD_KAPPA}} method sets the strength of the bias potential \kappa. The unit of \kappa is \textrm{eV}/(\textrm{unit} \,\, \textrm{ of }\,\, Q)^2.
