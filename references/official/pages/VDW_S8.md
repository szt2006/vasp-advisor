Description: {{TAG|VDW_S8}} sets the scaling s_{8} of the dipole-quadrupole dispersion of the DFT-D3 and DFT-D4 methods.
----
{{TAG|VDW_S8}} allows to set the scaling s_{8} of the dipole-quadrupole dispersion of the DFT-D3 and DFT-D4 ({{TAG|IVDW}}=13) methods. {{TAG|VDW_S8}} can be used for both implementations of DFT-D3: DFT-D3 ({{TAG|IVDW}}=11 or 12) and simple-DFT-D3 ({{TAG|IVDW}}=15).
