Description: The number of subdivisions of each RESPA step used in the integration step used in propagation of thermostat variables in the Nosé-Hoover chain thermostat. 

----
The RESPA steps used in in propagation of thermostat variables in the Nosé-Hoover chain thermostat are treated by Suzuki-Yoshida scheme, whereby each step is subdivided further into {{TAG|NHC_NS}} parts. First, fourth, and sixth order schemes with, 1, 3, and 7 steps, respectively, are supported.
