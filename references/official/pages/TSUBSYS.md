{{TAGDEF|TSUBSYS|[real array]}}

Description: {{TAG|TSUBSYS}} sets the temperatures for the atomic subsystems in calculations with multiple Anderson thermostats (in case VASP was compiled with -Dtbdyn).
----
Up to three user-defined atomic subsystems may be coupled with independent Andersen thermostats ({{TAG|MDALGO}}=13).

The simulation temperature for the atomic subsystems is set by means of the {{TAG|TSUBSYS}} tag (one has to specify one number for each subsystem).
