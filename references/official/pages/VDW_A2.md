Description: {{TAG|VDW_A2}} sets the offset a_{2} of the critical radii in the rational Becke-Johnson damping function of the DFT-D3 and DFT-D4 methods.
----
{{TAG|VDW_A2}} allows to set the offset a_{2} of the critical radii in the rational Becke-Johnson damping function:
*DFT-D3 ({{TAG|IVDW}}=12).
*simple-DFT-D3 ({{TAG|IVDW}}=15 with {{TAG|SDFTD3_DAMPING}}=rational, mrational, or optimizedpower).
*DFT-D4 ({{TAG|IVDW}}=13).
