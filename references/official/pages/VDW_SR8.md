{{TAG|VDW_SR8}} allows to set the radii scaling s_{r,8} in the dipole-quadrupole zero-damping function of the DFT-D3 method. {{TAG|VDW_SR8}} can be used for both implementations of DFT-D3: DFT-D3 ({{TAG|IVDW}}=11) and simple-DFT-D3 ({{TAG|IVDW}}=15 with {{TAG|SDFTD3_DAMPING}}=zero or mzero).
