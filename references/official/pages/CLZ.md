Description: {{TAG|CLZ}} selects a fractional or the full electron charge for the core hole in the core-excitation calculations with {{TAG|ICORELEVEL}}=2.
----
In SCH {{TAG|CLZ}} can be used to introduce a fraction of the core hole. For example, a half-core hole can be created to simulate a transition state in the X-ray excitation process.
