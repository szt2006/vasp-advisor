Description: {{TAG|EVENONLYGW}} allows to restrict the k-points in the evaluation of response functions (in {{TAG|GW calculations}}) to even values.

----

{{TAG|EVENONLYGW}}=*.TRUE.* is only of limited use and restricts the summation to k-points with n_{1}+n_{2}+n_{3} being even (\Gamma point and from there on every second k-point included).
